>>> EMPIEZA AQUÍ >>>

A KPI card should answer three questions at once: what is the number, how
does it compare, and where is it heading. KPI Card Pro answers all three in
the space of a tile — and now puts up to three supporting metrics beside the
headline, so "Sales 5.2M" arrives with Margin, Orders and Average ticket
next to it.

And it compares by itself. Comparing against the previous period normally
means a date table, DATEADD and one measure per KPI. Bind your date field to
Trend and the card reads the comparison off the series it already draws:
the latest month against the previous one, or the rolling twelve months
against the twelve before them.

WHAT IS NEW IN 2.0
• Secondary KPIs — up to three measures beside the main value, each with its
  own variance. They step aside below a card width you set, so the headline
  is never squeezed.
• Three ways to read the number — the total, the latest point of the trend,
  or the last N points against the N before.
• A period label that prints what is being compared: "Dec 2025 vs Nov 2025".

FREE
The complete card: value, variance against a prior period with its arrow and
the invert logic for costs and churn, target, images, display units,
cross-filtering, bookmarks, keyboard navigation and high contrast.

PRO
The trend line, Small Multiples — one card per category, up to fifty —, the
Secondary KPIs, the comparison modes, prefix and suffix, the variance pill
and its colours, extra tooltip measures, and the card styling.

Try it before you buy: while you edit without a licence, every Pro feature
you turn on is drawn working under a "Pro preview" watermark. Reading view
shows the free result, so a published report never uses a feature you have
not paid for.

NO NETWORK ACCESS
The visual makes no HTTP requests, stores nothing and needs no companion
service. Images must already be in your model as Base64 — links to images on
a web server are rejected on purpose, which is what makes that claim true.
