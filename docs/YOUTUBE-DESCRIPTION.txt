DESCRIPCIÓN LARGA
----------------------------------------------------------------
KPI Card Pro turns a single measure into a card that actually says
something: the number, how it compares with the prior period, the target,
and the trend behind it. Drop a category field in and the card becomes a
grid — one card per channel, product or region, each with its own line.

In this video:
• Building the card from a single measure
• Prior period variance, with the arrow and the comparison underneath
• The trend line: area, line or bars, over any date or period axis
• The target drawn period by period, so you see where you fell short
• One card per category with Small Multiples
• A logo per category from a Base64 image column
• What the Pro plan unlocks, and what stays free

FREE
The complete card: value, prior-period variance, target, display units,
label and card styling, all variance colours and the invert logic for
metrics where lower is better, images, tooltips, cross-filtering, keyboard
navigation and high contrast.

PRO
The trend line inside the card, and Small Multiples — up to 50 cards from a
category field, each with its own line. Plus prefix and suffix, the
variance pill, extra tooltip measures, and the card border and padding
controls.

PRIVACY
No network calls of any kind. All calculation happens inside Power BI, and
images must be embedded in your model as Base64 — external URLs are
rejected on purpose.
