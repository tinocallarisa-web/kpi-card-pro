KPI Card Pro 2.5.0.0 - GUID kpiCardProTCViz

FILTER OUT (1180.2.2.3, flagged on 2.1 and 2.4) - HOW TO SEE IT
The visual filters outwards. Open the sample .pbix:
1. Page 1 "Filter out": click a Small Multiples card (one per channel). The
   native bar chart beside it filters to that channel; other cards dim.
2. Page 2 "Trend": click a point of the line. The native table filters to that
   month. Ctrl+click adds; a click on empty space clears.
3. A single card with no Small Multiples and no Trend has no category to filter
   by, like the native Card.
Edit mode without a licence draws Small Multiples and Trend under the Pro
preview watermark; the clicks work there too.
